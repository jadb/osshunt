## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Neural Search combines keyword and semantic retrieval for queries that need meaning as well as exact terms. [1](https://www.algolia.com/pricing)
2. Visual merchandising rules, synonyms and ranking controls let search teams curate results. [1](https://www.algolia.com/pricing)
3. Search analytics and A/B testing support measuring relevance changes against user outcomes. [1](https://www.algolia.com/pricing)
