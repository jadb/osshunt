## Defensibility assessments

- Recognizable sketch style, shape libraries and an embedding ecosystem support adoption; open editor/export formats reduce exclusive lock-in. [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/excalidraw/excalidraw/master/README.md)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Hand-drawn-style shapes produce informal explanatory sketches quickly. [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/excalidraw/excalidraw/master/README.md)
2. Shape libraries and bound labeled arrows speed reusable technical diagrams. [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/excalidraw/excalidraw/master/README.md)
3. PNG/SVG and editable JSON export move drawings between documents and future edits. [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/excalidraw/excalidraw/master/README.md)
