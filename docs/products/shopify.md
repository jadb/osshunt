## Defensibility assessments

- Its app ecosystem and combined payments, checkout and retail workflows create complementary-network and switching-cost advantages. [1](https://www.shopify.com/pricing)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Unified online, POS and social-channel operations help merchants manage selling across customer touchpoints. [1](https://www.shopify.com/pricing)
2. Integrated checkout, payment methods and order tools connect storefront conversion with fulfillment workflows. [1](https://www.shopify.com/pricing)
3. POS inventory and customer tools connect physical retail activity to the ecommerce catalog. [1](https://www.shopify.com/pos)
