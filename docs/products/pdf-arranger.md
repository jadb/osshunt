## Defensibility assessments

- Focused thumbnail-based page manipulation is useful but readily replicable; technical moat appears limited. [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pdfarranger/pdfarranger/main/README.md)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Visual page thumbnails support rearrangement without specifying page-number ranges. [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pdfarranger/pdfarranger/main/README.md)
2. Crop and rotate operations fix scanned-page layout in the arrangement workflow. [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pdfarranger/pdfarranger/main/README.md)
3. Combining and splitting pages in one interface covers document assembly without a full content editor. [1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pdfarranger/pdfarranger/main/README.md)
