## Defensibility assessments

- A unified employee record spanning hiring, time off, compensation and reporting can make mature HR workflows costly to migrate. [1](https://www.bamboohr.com/pricing/)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Employee records, custom reporting and approvals connect HR data to day-to-day information changes. [1](https://www.bamboohr.com/pricing/)
2. Candidate records, posting and offer templates keep hiring activity close to employee onboarding. [1](https://www.bamboohr.com/pricing/)
3. Elite compensation planning, salary bands and Mercer benchmarks support structured pay reviews. [1](https://www.bamboohr.com/pricing/)
