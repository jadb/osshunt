1. On-screen drawing, shapes and text explain steps while recording. [1](https://github.com/alyssaxuu/screenity)
2. Live blur and cursor spotlight guide attention while obscuring sensitive page content. [1](https://github.com/alyssaxuu/screenity)
3. Trim/crop/audio editing and MP4/GIF/WebM export prepare local clips for reuse. [1](https://github.com/alyssaxuu/screenity)
