## Defensibility assessments

- Combined mail, calendar, contacts and task habits across devices can make replacing Outlook disruptive for established users. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/outlook/email-and-calendar-software-microsoft-outlook)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Multiple email accounts and calendars in one interface help coordinate separate inboxes. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/outlook/email-and-calendar-software-microsoft-outlook)
2. Integrated contacts and to-do lists connect correspondence to daily planning. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/outlook/email-and-calendar-software-microsoft-outlook)
3. Paid Basic mailbox security and expanded 100 GB mailbox help manage substantial personal email archives. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/outlook/outlook-email-plans-and-pricing)
