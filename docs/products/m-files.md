## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Context and metadata relationships organize documents around business information rather than folder location. [1](https://www.m-files.com/about/)
2. Native Microsoft365 experiences let teams manage governed documents within familiar Office collaboration tools. [1](https://www.m-files.com/editions/)
3. Graphical workflows and Aino assistance combine approvals, lifecycle control and contextual document tasks. [1](https://www.m-files.com/editions/)
