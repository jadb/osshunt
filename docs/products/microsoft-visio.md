## Defensibility assessments

- A large diagram shape library and established Microsoft diagram workflows create a practical migration barrier for heavy Visio users. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/visio/visio-plans-and-pricing)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Dedicated Azure, AWS, ERD and UML shapes help model technical systems precisely. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/visio/visio-plans-and-pricing)
2. Advanced organization-chart layouts with employee photos and fields suit structured people diagrams. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/visio/visio-plans-and-pricing)
3. Layers, containers and shape data support richer diagrams than simple sketch canvases. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/visio/visio-plans-and-pricing)
