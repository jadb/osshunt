## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Broad integrations and shared dashboards let operators investigate infrastructure alongside application signals. [1](https://www.datadoghq.com/pricing/)
2. Outlier, anomaly and forecast monitors help surface abnormal behavior beyond fixed thresholds. [1](https://www.datadoghq.com/pricing/)
3. Host/container maps and Live Processes support investigation of changing infrastructure. [1](https://www.datadoghq.com/pricing/)
