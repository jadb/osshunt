## Defensibility assessments

- Its established merchant/module ecosystem and European commerce specialization create localized implementation and extension advantages. [1](https://prestashop.com/about-us/)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. A unified one-page checkout reduces the number of checkout steps merchants need to configure. [1](https://prestashop.com/about-us/)
2. Localized payments and multi-currency selling support merchants serving multiple national markets. [1](https://prestashop.com/prestashop-offers/)
3. Catalog connections to social networks and marketing modules link product merchandising to acquisition channels. [1](https://prestashop.com/prestashop-offers/)
