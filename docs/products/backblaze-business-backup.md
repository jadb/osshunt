## Defensibility assessments

- Owned storage infrastructure plus endpoint adoption and backup history create operational scale and migration costs; underlying file backup remains competitive. [1](https://ir.backblaze.com/overview/default.aspx) [2](https://www.backblaze.com/cloud-backup/business)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Automatic Mac/Windows backup includes user-generated files and attached external drives by default. [1](https://www.backblaze.com/cloud-backup/pricing) [2](https://www.backblaze.com/cloud-backup/business)
2. Centralized group administration manages multiple computers and IT/end-user restore privileges. [1](https://www.backblaze.com/cloud-backup/pricing)
3. Enterprise Control adds backup lockdown and restricted restore access for centrally governed recovery. [1](https://www.backblaze.com/cloud-backup/pricing)
