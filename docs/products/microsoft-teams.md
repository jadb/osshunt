## Defensibility assessments

- Persistent chats, shared files and Microsoft 365 workflows tie organizational collaboration into an established workspace. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-teams/enterprise)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Chats and channels combine shared content, Loop and OneNote pages for continuing team context. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-teams/enterprise)
2. Meetings and event features connect daily collaboration to organized group calls and webinars on eligible plans. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-teams/enterprise)
3. Teams Phone can connect internal communication with external telephone numbers using separately licensed calling services. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-teams/enterprise)
