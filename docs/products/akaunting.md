## Defensibility assessments

- Modular financial workflows and third-party app extensions can increase customization switching costs, though key accounting functions depend on add-ons. [1](https://akaunting.com/about)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Recurring payment reminders and multi-currency records help coordinate invoice collection across customers. [1](https://akaunting.com/plans-on-premise)
2. Multiple bank accounts and bank-transaction imports support consolidating cash activity. [1](https://akaunting.com/plans-on-premise)
3. Customer, vendor and product records plus an app marketplace allow bookkeeping workflows to expand into adjacent operations. [1](https://akaunting.com/about)
