## Defensibility assessments

- Large collaborative installed base and accumulated team-folder/link workflows create network and switching effects, tempered by competing file-sync standards and exportability. [1](https://investors.dropbox.com/node/13601/html) [2](https://www.dropbox.com/plans)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Cross-device synchronization and shared team folders support persistent collaborative file access. [1](https://www.dropbox.com/plans)
2. Deleted-file restoration and plan-dependent history provide recovery from accidental changes. [1](https://www.dropbox.com/plans)
3. File transfers, PDF editing and e-signatures combine common document handoff tasks. [1](https://www.dropbox.com/plans)
