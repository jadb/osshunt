## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Keyword/vector retrieval plus inference/reranking supports both traditional search and AI retrieval. [1](https://www.elastic.co/pricing/serverless-search)
2. Kibana exploration and dashboards connect indexed data to interactive investigation. [1](https://www.elastic.co/pricing/)
3. Serverless separates search and ingest compute so workloads can be tuned independently. [1](https://www.elastic.co/pricing/serverless-search)
