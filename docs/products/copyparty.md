## Defensibility assessments

- Integrated protocol/media features offer niche usability differentiation; a portable MIT implementation provides limited exclusive defensibility. [1](https://github.com/9001/copyparty)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Resumable browser uploads and content deduplication support unreliable connections and repeated large transfers. [1](https://github.com/9001/copyparty)
2. WebDAV, SFTP, FTP and TFTP expose files through multiple client protocols. [1](https://github.com/9001/copyparty)
3. Media indexing, thumbnails and browser playback combine file delivery with media browsing. [1](https://github.com/9001/copyparty)
