## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Row/column permissions and tenant segregation allow one analytics application to serve different data audiences. [1](https://www.metabase.com/pricing)
2. Interactive embedding and branding controls integrate analytics into customer-facing products. [1](https://www.metabase.com/pricing)
3. Usage analytics, auditing and caching controls make analytical workflows observable and tunable. [1](https://www.metabase.com/pricing)
