## Defensibility assessments

- Backup formats, workload agents and integrated protection/management workflows create switching effort, reinforced by an established MSP ecosystem. [1](https://eqtgroup.com/about/current-portfolio/acronis) [2](https://www.acronis.com/en/products/cyber-protect/)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Full-image and file-level protection cover physical and virtual workloads alongside application data. [1](https://www.acronis.com/en/products/cyber-protect/)
2. Integrated malware scanning and safe recovery help avoid restoring compromised backups; edition restrictions apply. [1](https://www.acronis.com/en/products/cyber-protect/purchasing/)
3. Central backup plans, tape support and storage snapshots cover more complex recovery environments in Advanced editions. [1](https://www.acronis.com/en/products/cyber-protect/purchasing/)
