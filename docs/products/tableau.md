## Defensibility assessments

- Established visualization workbooks, analytical skills and reusable data preparation flows make migration costly for existing analytics teams. [1](https://www.tableau.com/why-tableau/what-is-tableau)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Visual exploration lets analysts discover relationships while building interactive analytical views. [1](https://www.tableau.com/why-tableau/what-is-tableau)
2. Prep Builder and Desktop connect data preparation with analysis in the same product family. [1](https://www.tableau.com/pricing)
3. Pulse metrics help business users follow important measures without manually opening every dashboard. [1](https://www.tableau.com/pricing)
