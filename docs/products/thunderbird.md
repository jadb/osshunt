## Defensibility assessments

- Long-lived cross-platform mail workflows and an add-on ecosystem create familiarity and extensibility advantages, while standard email protocols keep the moat limited. [1](https://www.thunderbird.net/en-US/desktop/)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. A unified inbox manages multiple mail accounts without separate browser sessions. [1](https://www.thunderbird.net/en-US/desktop/)
2. Calendar, contacts and tasks connect email to personal planning in one app. [1](https://www.thunderbird.net/en-US/desktop/)
3. Add-ons extend the desktop client for specialized workflows beyond the included interface. [1](https://www.thunderbird.net/en-US/desktop/)
