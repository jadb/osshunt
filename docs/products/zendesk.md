## Defensibility assessments

- Broad ticketing, knowledge, voice and workflow ecosystem creates process and integration switching costs. [1](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Omnichannel routing assigns tickets using agent capacity, skills and ticket priority. [1](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/)
2. Action Builder connects multi-step resolution flows to third-party systems. [1](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/)
3. Knowledge base and reporting bring self-service answers and operational trends into the support workflow. [1](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/)
