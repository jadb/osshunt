## Defensibility assessments

- A painting-specific brush ecosystem and artist workflows provide specialization relative to general photo editors. [1](https://krita.org/en/features/)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Multiple brush engines and stabilization provide fine control for digital painting. [1](https://krita.org/en/features/)
2. SVG vector tools and text templates support comic panels and speech bubbles. [1](https://krita.org/en/features/)
3. Custom workspaces, dockers and shortcuts adapt the interface to illustration workflows. [1](https://krita.org/en/features/)
