## Defensibility assessments

- Application SDKs, identity extensions and accumulated login-flow integrations can increase switching work; standards-based protocols retain alternative options. [1](https://auth0.com/pricing)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Universal Login and passwordless/passkey methods provide a configurable application login experience. [1](https://auth0.com/pricing)
2. Organizations and per-organization roles model B2B customer accounts and access. [1](https://auth0.com/pricing)
3. Actions, Forms and marketplace extensions customize identity flows without replacing the authentication platform. [1](https://auth0.com/pricing)
