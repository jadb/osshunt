## Defensibility assessments

- Combining regulated payments, card controls and approval/accounting workflows can create switching costs across finance and employee spending processes. [1](https://www.spendesk.com/pricing/)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Single-use, recurring and multi-use virtual cards let teams apply different controls to one-off purchases, subscriptions and projects. [1](https://www.spendesk.com/pricing/)
2. OCR, receipt matching and reminders reduce the manual work of collecting purchase evidence. [1](https://www.spendesk.com/pricing/)
3. Multi-entity controls and real-time budgets consolidate spending decisions across legal entities. [1](https://www.spendesk.com/pricing/)
