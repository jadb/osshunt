## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Interactive visual reports let business users explore governed metrics without authoring SQL for each view. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/products/power-bi)
2. OneLake integration brings cloud and on-premises data together for unified analysis. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/products/power-bi)
3. Eligible Fabric Copilot features turn natural-language questions into visualizations and reports. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/products/power-bi)
