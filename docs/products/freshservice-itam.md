## Defensibility assessments

- Asset, service and operations context in one platform can create workflow and data relationships that raise migration costs; value depends on accurate inventory. [1](https://www.freshworks.com/freshservice/itam/pricing/)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Discovery, CMDB and lifecycle management provide a common inventory of hardware and hybrid infrastructure. [1](https://www.freshworks.com/freshservice/itam/pricing/)
2. Software/Saa S discovery, normalization and contract/purchase-order tracking connect inventory to licensed usage. [1](https://www.freshworks.com/freshservice/itam/pricing/)
3. DCIM rack/cable views and application/business-service mapping show physical and logical dependencies. [1](https://www.freshworks.com/freshservice/itam/pricing/)
