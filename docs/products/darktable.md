## Defensibility assessments

- Specialized RAW processing expertise and accumulated styles/catalogs create workflow differentiation with a substantial learning investment. [1](https://www.darktable.org/about/)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Floating-point nondestructive RAW processing preserves edits until export. [1](https://www.darktable.org/about/)
2. Tethered camera capture joins shooting and photo development. [1](https://www.darktable.org/about/)
3. Lighttable catalog and extensible modules combine image management with detailed processing control. [1](https://www.darktable.org/about/)
