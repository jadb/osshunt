## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Marketing automation flows coordinate triggered customer journeys rather than isolated bulk newsletters. [1](https://mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/)
2. Predictive segmentation and multivariate testing help target and optimize campaigns. [1](https://mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/)
3. Commerce integrations and dynamic content connect product/customer data to personalized emails. [1](https://mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/)
