## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Slack Connect shared channels keep partner and customer discussions connected across organizations. [1](https://slack.com/pricing)
2. Huddles, canvases and lists combine live discussion, shared context and task tracking. [1](https://slack.com/pricing)
3. Workflow Builder and custom app integrations automate actions from conversations. [1](https://slack.com/pricing)
