## Defensibility assessments

- Widely shared booking links and a large integration ecosystem support distribution and embedded workflow switching costs; scheduling itself remains replicable. [1](https://calendly.com/about)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Round-robin scheduling and qualification routing distribute incoming meetings across a team. [1](https://calendly.com/pricing)
2. Multiple calendar conflict checks and automated reminders coordinate availability and reduce manual follow-up. [1](https://calendly.com/pricing)
3. Stripe and PayPal connections collect payments as part of the booking workflow. [1](https://calendly.com/pricing)
