## Defensibility assessments

- Joining meetings from existing Workspace documents and Calendar creates workflow switching costs for established Workspace teams. [1](https://workspace.google.com/products/meet/)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Joining directly from Docs, Sheets and Slides keeps a shared document and meeting connected. [1](https://workspace.google.com/products/meet/)
2. Live captions help participants follow spoken discussion in real time. [1](https://workspace.google.com/products/meet/)
3. Eligible plans combine noise reduction, polls and breakout rooms for more interactive group meetings. [1](https://workspace.google.com/products/meet/)
