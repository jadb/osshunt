## Defensibility assessments

- Existing Office files, identity policies and integrated business apps create meaningful organization-wide switching costs. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/business/microsoft-365-plans-and-pricing)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Desktop Word, Excel and PowerPoint in applicable plans serve advanced document, spreadsheet and presentation work. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/business/microsoft-365-plans-and-pricing)
2. Exchange email, calendar and SharePoint team sites connect communication with shared business content. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/business/microsoft-365-plans-and-pricing)
3. Bookings, Planner and Forms cover scheduling, task coordination and information collection inside the suite. [1](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/business/microsoft-365-plans-and-pricing)
