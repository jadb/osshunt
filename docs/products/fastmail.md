## Defensibility assessments

- Email, calendar and shared-address workflow habits create switching friction, although standard protocols limit proprietary lock-in. [1](https://www.fastmail.help/hc/en-us/articles/360058753254-Fastmail-features) [2](https://www.fastmail.com/company/values/)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Masked Email aliases help separate online account identities from the main inbox. [1](https://www.fastmail.help/hc/en-us/articles/360058753254-Fastmail-features)
2. Shared calendars, contacts and email addresses coordinate small business correspondence. [1](https://www.fastmail.help/hc/en-us/articles/360058753254-Fastmail-features)
3. Scheduled Send, Snooze and inbox rules help users manage follow-up timing and incoming mail. [1](https://www.fastmail.help/hc/en-us/articles/360058753254-Fastmail-features)
