## Defensibility assessments

- Large installed business base and credentials integrated into daily workflows may create switching costs; this is an assessment of adoption and integration depth. [1](https://1password.com/company) [2](https://1password.com/pricing/business)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Developer workflows combine SSH/Git signing with CLI and SDK integrations, useful for teams handling application credentials. [1](https://1password.com/pricing/business)
2. Role-based vault permissions allow shared credential access to follow team responsibilities. [1](https://1password.com/pricing/business)
3. Watchtower surfaces credential risks within the password-management workflow. [1](https://1password.com/pricing/business)
