## Defensibility assessments

- Its recognizable conversational interaction design and integrated marketing workflows create brand and workflow switching costs. [1](https://www.typeform.com/blog/typeform-secures-135-million-series-c-funding-led-by-sofina)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Conversational forms with conditional logic adapt the question path to each respondent. [1](https://www.typeform.com/pricing)
2. Drop-off and conversion analysis help teams find where respondents abandon a form. [1](https://www.typeform.com/pricing)
3. AI clarification and video answers support richer qualitative responses than ordinary static fields. [1](https://www.typeform.com/pricing)
