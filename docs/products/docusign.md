## Defensibility assessments

- Large recipient adoption and extensive business integrations reinforce trust familiarity and integration switching costs. [1](https://investor.docusign.com/overview/)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Reusable/shared templates and collaborative commenting support consistent agreement preparation across teams. [1](https://ecom.docusign.com/en-US/plans-and-pricing/esignature)
2. Bulk sending, web forms and payment collection automate high-volume signature workflows. [1](https://ecom.docusign.com/en-US/plans-and-pricing/esignature)
3. Delegated signing, custody transfer and audit trails support controlled handoffs and traceable agreement completion. [1](https://ecom.docusign.com/en-US/plans-and-pricing/esignature)
