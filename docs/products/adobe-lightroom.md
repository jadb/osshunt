## Defensibility assessments

- Photo catalogs, reusable presets and a cross-device editing workflow create accumulated workflow switching costs. [1](https://www.adobe.com/products/photoshop-lightroom.html)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Presets can be applied across photo sets for consistent batch edits. [1](https://www.adobe.com/products/photoshop-lightroom.html)
2. Photo organization and editing share a workflow instead of requiring a separate catalog. [1](https://www.adobe.com/products/photoshop-lightroom.html)
3. Desktop, mobile and web editing enable a continuous cross-device workflow. [1](https://www.adobe.com/products/photoshop-lightroom.html)
