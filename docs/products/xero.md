## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Bank reconciliation and document capture reduce manual entry for routine bookkeeping. [1](https://www.xero.com/us/pricing-plans/)
2. Established combines multi-currency, project time/cost tracking and employee expenses for internationally active service firms. [1](https://www.xero.com/us/pricing-plans/)
3. Cash-flow forecasts and customizable financial dashboards help translate accounting entries into planning decisions. [1](https://www.xero.com/us/pricing-plans/)
