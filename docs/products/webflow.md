## Defensibility assessments

- Visual design, structured CMS and governance bundled into one platform can create workflow and design-system switching costs. [1](https://webflow.com/company/about)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Visual layout and GSAP interaction tools let teams build responsive, animated experiences inside the website editor. [1](https://webflow.com/pricing)
2. CMS collections, custom fields and content APIs support structured content reused across pages and channels. [1](https://webflow.com/pricing)
3. Page branching, staged publishing and approvals enable teams to review changes before release. [1](https://webflow.com/pricing)
