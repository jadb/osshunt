## Defensibility assessments

- Established nonlinear editing workflows and community-maintained format integration create category-specific accumulated expertise. [1](https://kdenlive.org/)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. Nested sequences help manage complex video timelines. [1](https://kdenlive.org/)
2. Proxy clips and preview scaling keep large footage workable during editing. [1](https://kdenlive.org/)
3. Multicam and three-point editing support structured narrative assembly. [1](https://kdenlive.org/)
