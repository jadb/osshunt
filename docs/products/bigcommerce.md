## Defensibility assessments

- Established commerce integrations and combined B2B/B2C workflows create specialized integration and migration costs. [1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1626450/000095017025100557/bigc-20250630.htm)

## Three product reasons to consider it

1. B2B purchasing features support company buyer accounts and business-specific procurement workflows. [1](https://www.bigcommerce.com/solutions/b2b-ecommerce-platform/)
2. Multi-storefront capabilities let a merchant operate multiple brands or regions through one commerce platform. [1](https://www.bigcommerce.com/pricing/)
3. Headless storefront and integration APIs let merchants customize the buying experience around existing business systems. [1](https://www.bigcommerce.com/solutions/b2b-ecommerce-platform/)
