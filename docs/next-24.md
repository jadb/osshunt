# Suggested next 24 categories

Ordered by estimated potential business audience, from cross-functional use to specialist teams. This is a research backlog: candidate examples are not yet fully audited for licenses, health, paid feature gates or migration fit. The linked official sources establish candidate scope only. Adjacent ranks are judgment calls, not comparable user counts.

| Priority | Category | Paid targets | Candidates to investigate | Audience and scope |
