# Product profile interpretation

Enrichment covers all catalog records, including the clearly labelled S/U alternative candidates. Check `research/enrichment-manifest.json` for actual coverage and completion status. Early checkpoints remain subject to source review.

- **Organization age:** distinguish company incorporation, project start and product launch. Calendar-year differences are approximate; project age is not represented as years in business. Public subsidiary identifies its listed parent separately. A public benefit corporation is not automatically publicly traded or nonprofit.
- **Funding:** bootstrap requires affirmative evidence. No funding announcement found means unknown. Named investors may be historical; investor lists are not current cap tables. Donors, grants, customers and acquirers do not automatically become equity investors.
- **Pricing:** entry, explicitly highlighted popular, team/business and enterprise roles. Most-popular status needs a vendor designation. Unknown base amounts and quoted subscription amounts stay null. Monthly means monthly billing; annual is a full annual total, including disclosed multiplication of annual-billed monthly rates. Usage prices have a separate field and usage denominator (`usageUnit`, falling back to the base unit only when identical). A published metered add-on may accompany a quoted subscription; quoted base amounts remain null. Usage rates never become invented annual subscriptions. Seat minimums, currencies, usage denominators, first-year promotions and scope remain explicit. Free open code and paid cloud/service offers are separate scopes; prices do not imply feature parity.
- **Sponsors:** financial or documented in-kind corporate sponsors. If no corporate sponsor was detected and a verified business operates the project, that business appears as business-steward fallback. Investors and customers are not automatically sponsors. No sponsor found in reviewed sources is not proof none exists.
- **Self-operation:** default documented production route plus supported runtime/database/service alternatives. Required, optional, conditional and one-of choices remain distinct. Database alternative groups are not cumulative installation requirements. Desktop and library projects retain local/runtime requirements without pretending they are server products. Hardware minima, recommendations and unknowns remain distinct.
- **Installation:** verified first-party or officially linked options. Browser one-click, marketplace deployment and shell one-command paths have separate labels. Manual Compose instructions alone do not qualify as one-click. The derived `hasVerifiedOneClick` flag counts verified browser/marketplace options; false alongside unknown status means no verified option recorded, not proof none exists.
- **Moats and selection reasons:** editorial assessments supported by linked facts, not measured competitive advantage. Exactly three product reasons per record; hosting, support, price and fundraising are not product reasons. Evaluate edition gates and mapping limitations before choosing.

Structured records are `research/profiles/<id>.json`, merged into the existing `products` collection by stable ID. Product claim ledgers and chronological research steps live under `research/profile-claims/` and `research/profile-audits/`. Public sources are cited near facts in generated profile pages. Website bodies are not universally archived; dated URLs and page locators preserve provenance without claiming replayable snapshots.
