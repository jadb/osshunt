# Digital Asset Management

Catalog, search, govern and share photos, video, brand files and other media with structured metadata and access controls.

Audience: Marketing and creative teams, brand managers, media librarians, archives and cultural institutions.. A recurring function across industries, primarily used by teams managing reusable media collections rather than every employee.
