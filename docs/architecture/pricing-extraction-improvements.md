# Pricing extraction and discovery improvements

Implement the improvements identified by the retained-source audits before another held-out cohort. Existing six-calendar-month pricing reservations, 30-day historical capture matching, immutable bodies and explicit confirmation remain in force.

## Sources and provenance

Inspect a verified landing body for supported pricing structure as well as following eligible pricing links. A landing-source observation uses `landing-page` provenance; its pricing and landing URL, replay, capture date, body hash, digest and transport identity must agree. Its discovery locator identifies a retained plan/offer/feature field rather than inventing a pricing anchor. Landing proposals use the canonical product scope.

Dedicated-page observations retain archived-link or current-source-hint provenance and independent source dates. Partial landing structures with no priced offer or explicit named quote structure remain fallback candidates while dedicated sources are checked. Generic application metadata or an aggregate zero does not establish a meaningful free tier. Proposed observations remain partial and never publish without review.

## Structures and gaps

Use inert parsed HTML and JSON-LD. Generic heading/group relationships supplement existing explicit cards/tables and adapters; no hostname-specific rules for the new regression cases. Keep exact decimal strings and source locators. Bind billing frequency, commitment, denominator, limits, discounts and add-ons only where source grouping supports the relationship. Preserve unknown fields, conflicting interpretations and duplicate billing representations instead of inventing plan names or identities.

Extraction diagnostics distinguish static pricing, embedded pricing, partial structures, quote-only structures, client-rendered failure/fallback content and unsupported templates. Quote-only does not mean zero; a free trial is separate from a recurring free plan. Paid-price placeholders remain unknown. Large or unavailable captures remain source gaps, independently of parser output. Bound extraction output and checkpoint diagnostics; report truncation rather than implying completeness.

## Rejected navigation and reviewed aliases

Discovery returns its existing accepted-link array and optionally records safe rejected pricing navigation with exact URL, source locator, label, provenance and reason. Retain at most 100 diagnostic entries and report total/retained/omitted counts. Unsafe identifiers are excluded from diagnostics. Neither a missing candidate nor an empty retained rejection list proves no pricing link exists.

Reviewed aliases in `research/pricing-aliases.json` authorize exact target URLs, including their queries. Each association records an actual archived canonical landing capture, source SHA-256, anchor locator/label, scope and observed locale. Aliases become available only at or after their evidence capture date. They do not widen an entire host or path prefix. Alias evidence dates join the observation's availability chain; bundle or shared-product pricing retains its explicit scope. A landing observation cannot masquerade as an alias-discovered source.

## Reassessment

Test previous cached sources without network access and preserve the original unsuccessful results. Freeze implementation/catalog hashes before collecting the next 20 untried paid catalog tools in audience order. Independently review every nonempty extraction, including schema-valid proposals, and inspect empty outputs for source or discovery gaps. Measure development coverage separately from held-out performance. A zero-fetch replay and offline integrity check verify process behavior; they do not establish factual price accuracy.
