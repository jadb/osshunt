# Historical pricing evidence

As someone comparing a paid product with an OSS alternative, I can inspect confirmed past plans, prices and feature limits alongside archived images, retaining the actual source and capture date.

Acceptance:

- Pricing checks and observations use six calendar months; image history retains 30 days.
- Archived landing links establish pricing provenance; current-source hints stay labelled.
- Exact dates, immutable hashes, billing terms and unknown values remain in structured evidence.
- Only explicitly confirmed observations publish; missing sources remain gaps.
- Image association excludes future evidence; pricing works without screenshots.
- Visits share reservations, cooldowns and resumable inventory; public pages cannot request arbitrary URLs or read private bodies.
- Concurrent confirmations preserve all reviewed history; restart/redeployment publishes updates.

Verification: calendar/archive/extraction/scope/review/service/store/component and HTTP tests; browser gallery/iframe smoke; Worker dry-run. Positive live pricing examples remain outstanding as detailed in the source audit.
