# Logo sources

Brand marks shown beside product names identify the products only. Trademarks belong to their owners; no endorsement is implied.

- **svgl.app** marks: exact product or brand matches, light variant. Files `site/public/logos/*.svg` without the `si-` prefix.
- **Simple Icons** (CC0-1.0 artwork), pinned at 16.34.0, filled with the brand's published colour: files prefixed `si-`.
- Mapping from product ID to file: `site/src/lib/logos.ts`. Only exact product or owning-brand matches are used; a parent brand's mark is not substituted for a different product (Google Workspace, SAP Concur, Zoho Mail, Frappe HR have none).
- Remaining products have no mark and show text only. Official brand-kit marks have not been sourced.
