## Process findings

- Firebase and Algolia provide service usage schedules, not a fixed product subscription fee. Preserve per-service denominators, free quotas, bucket/region conditions and unknown currency; Firebase rates explicitly starting August 2025 remain dated raw facts rather than effective June prices.
- Heroku families remain separate. Approximate hourly representations and monthly caps/ranges describe the same resources; Eco’s flat shared-hours fee is distinct. Offer counts do not count independent subscriptions.
- Vercel prices bind to the actual Pro/Enterprise service-table columns, with plan seats and optional charges kept separate.
- Retool’s animated headline digits do not establish amounts. Literal annual Cloud comparison rows supply prices; external-user bands and workflow capacity retain their conditions. No self-hosted price inferred.
- Insomnia’s hidden savings label is excluded from the plan name; explicit Enterprise annual billing and optional request packs retain their own terms.
- Postman monthly and annual variants stay qualified, and paid add-on availability is not an included plan capability. Bulk monthly resource packs and additional per-call rates remain separate source representations.
- Pingdom’s selected pricing HTML fails its archived payload digest. A second landing link targets a PDF; the HTML-only collector does not establish whether an archived PDF exists. The verified landing supports only a 30-day trial.
- Elastic Cloud’s explicit dated Serverless pricing link receives one exact alias after the frozen baseline. Supplemental June 4 HTML fails the payload digest and is not retained or used for prices. Its canonical landing supports a trial; no renewal fee or duration invented.
- Thirty-eight HTTP 200 responses are transport outcomes, not usable-evidence or pricing-success counts. The frozen baseline has three schema-valid proposals; source review supplies supported manual records without claiming reusable parser coverage.

Collection completion, retained sources, schema-valid parsing and reviewed numerical prices remain separate outcomes. Missing captures/replays cannot be repaired by extraction code.
